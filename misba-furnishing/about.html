Our Legacy

Three decades of making houses feel like home.

For over 30 years, Misba Furnishing has been helping families transform their spaces with curtains, blinds, wallpapers, sofas, mattresses, and customized furnishing solutions.

What started in Shivamogga has grown through trust, quality, and word-of-mouth recommendations to serve customers across Karnataka. Every space we create is thoughtfully customized to match our customers’ taste, lifestyle, and vision.

With years of experience and thousands of happy customers, we continue to believe that the best spaces are the ones that truly feel like your own.
